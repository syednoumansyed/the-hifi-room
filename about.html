About The HiFi Room

We buy, test, clean, and sell vintage hi-fi.

Based in Bandra West, Mumbai, we buy pre-owned vintage audio gear, test all functions, clean and service what is needed, document cosmetic condition honestly, and sell gear as-is with pan-India delivery.

Straightforward & Honest

Buying vintage audio can be frustrating when sellers don't disclose faults or describe broken gear as "mint". At The HiFi Room, we keep things simple and honest:

  • We test every device: We check power, inputs, phono stages, tone controls, and speaker outputs.
  • We clean and service: We clean dust, spray Caig DeoxIT on scratchy volume pots and switches, and replace worn belts or burned-out dial lamps.
  • We describe gear as it is: If a unit has a scratch on the side or a minor quirk, we photograph it and write it in the listing.

Safe, Heavy-Duty Packaging

Vintage gear is heavy and delicate. We pack turntables with platters and tonearms secured, tube amplifiers with valves individually wrapped in bubble foam, and heavy receivers in double-walled boxes or reinforced wooden crating with tracking across India.
