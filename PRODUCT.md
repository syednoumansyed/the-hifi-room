# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Node.js backend (Express) + REST API + Static/Dynamic Responsive Frontend (HTML5, modern CSS3 with custom properties, Vanilla ES6+ JS) with zero unnecessary build overhead, instant startup, local database/JSON store, and complete admin asset & product management.

## Users

1. **Audiophiles & Music Lovers**: Audio enthusiasts looking for tested, cleaned, functional vintage audio gear (turntables, amplifiers, receivers, tape decks, speakers) with transparent descriptions and no hidden surprises.
2. **Sellers & Gear Owners**: Individuals who want a straightforward, no-hassle cash sale for their unused vintage audio equipment without dealing with unverified online classifieds or waiting on consignment.
3. **Gear Seekers**: Buyers looking for specific vintage models and wanting someone to track one down for them.
4. **Store Operators**: Managing inventory, testing notes, intake leads, and customer inquiries.

## Product Purpose

"The HiFi Room" buys, tests, cleans, and sells curated vintage audio equipment in Mumbai and ships across India. We test every unit, fix common issues when needed (cleaning scratchy pots, replacing worn belts/lamps), document cosmetic wear and quirks honestly, and sell gear as-is with clear condition grading.

## Positioning

"Tested, cleaned, and honestly documented." We keep things transparent: we inspect and test all gear, disclose all cosmetic wear and minor faults up front, clean and service what we can, and pack everything securely for pan-India delivery. No overpromising—just solid, working vintage audio you can trust.

## Operating Context

- Workshop & showroom in Bandra West, Mumbai.
- Equipment testing setup: audio source testing, channel balance verification, pot cleaning, physical inspection.
- Direct cash buyouts from sellers.
- Custom reinforced packaging for tracked pan-India shipping.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Storefront**:
  - Home / Portal (`index.html`) highlighting featured inventory, categories, testing checklist, trade-in form, and recently sold ledger.
  - Shop Catalog (`shop.html`) with category, grade, and price filtering.
  - Product Detail View (`product.html`) with multi-angle photos, condition notes, verified testing checklist, known faults disclosure, and offer / WhatsApp inquiry.
  - Sell / Appraisal Intake (`sell.html`) with straightforward 4-step submission form.
  - Equipment Finder (`find.html`) to request specific vintage gear.
  - Grading Standard (`grading.html`) explaining practical condition grades (A+, A, B, C).
  - Recently Sold (`sold.html`) documenting historical sales.
  - About Us (`about.html`) explaining how we test, clean, and ship gear.
- **Admin Console**:
  - Live inventory management, status updater, seller leads review, sourcing requests, and store configuration.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name**: THE HIFI ROOM
- **Tone**: Honest, down-to-earth, respectful of vintage audio heritage, straightforward.
- **Aesthetic**: Warm analogue parchment, clean tactile typography, museum-grade photography of vintage hardware.

## Product Principles

1. **Don't Overpromise**: Describe gear accurately as it is. If something has a quirk, say it clearly.
2. **Every Unit Tested & Cleaned**: Check all inputs, outputs, switches, and playback before listing.
3. **Fair Direct Buyouts**: Make selling easy with direct cash purchase.
4. **Careful Packaging**: Vintage equipment is delicate; pack it with heavy-duty protection.
