Our Testing Protocol

How we test and prepare every single device

Bandra West Workshop
01. Inspection & Cleaning

Chassis opened, dust safely removed, fuses and power cables inspected for electrical safety.

02. Pot & Switch DeoxIT

Scratchy volume dials, tone pots, and input switches cleaned and lubricated so controls turn quietly.

03. All Inputs & Outputs

Tested with real turntables on Phono inputs, DACs on Aux, tape loops, and left/right speaker outputs A & B.

04. 4-Hour Burn-In Test

Played continuously with music for 4+ hours to make sure channels don't drop and the unit stays stable.
