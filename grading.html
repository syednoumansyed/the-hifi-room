Practical Condition Rubric

How we grade and describe every unit

We believe in honest, straightforward descriptions. Every piece of vintage audio has a history—some look brand new, while others carry light cosmetic patina. Here is exactly what our condition grades mean.

Our Workshop Checklist
01

Visual & Interior Check

Chassis opened, dust cleared, fuses and power cables inspected for safety.

02

DeoxIT Pot Cleaning

Volume controls, balance, bass/treble pots, and input switches cleaned and lubricated.

03

Audio Playback Test

Tested with turntables, Aux DAC inputs, and real speakers across left and right channels.

04

Honest Patina Notes

Any scratches, scuffs, or quirks photographed and listed in the product description.

Condition Tiers Explained

Grade A+ · Collector / Near-Mint

Indistinguishable from new in regular lighting. Flawless faceplate, all lettering sharp, zero notable marks. Fully tested and working perfectly.

Best for: Collectors wanting pristine museum-grade vintage audio.

Grade A · Excellent / Light Use

Clean, well-maintained unit with only minor hairline marks on top or rear cover. Faceplate and knobs look great. 100% tested and fully functional.

Best for: Everyday audiophiles looking for great looks and solid performance.

Grade B · Very Good / Honest Patina

A well-loved classic with visible character: minor edge wear or faint scratches, but zero compromise on internal functionality. Controls cleaned and quiet.

Best for: Buyers who value authentic vintage sound at a fair price.

Grade C · Fair / Minor Quirks (Sold As-Is)

Noticeable cosmetic marks or minor non-critical quirks (e.g. replaced non-original knob, small veneer chip) priced accordingly. Plays music properly.

Best for: Budget setups or casual listening spaces.
